KETS ADDITIONAL STRATEGIES
===========================
Implemented as an additive strategy engine in bot.py.

Available independent candidates:
- Trend Following
- Breakout
- Mean Reversion
- Momentum
- Price Action
- Support & Resistance
- Supply & Demand
- VWAP
- Moving Average Cross
- MACD
- RSI
- Bollinger Bands
- Fibonacci
- Scalping
- News / Volatility (measured volatility expansion; no news calendar claim)

The existing KETS core strategy, Strong Reversal and SMC logic are preserved.
Additional strategies are sent as independent strategy candidates to the KETS
website. They are OFF by default in the KETS Auto Trader controls.
